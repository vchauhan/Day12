# SECTION 1

# ATS-Friendly Cover Letter

**Dear Hiring Manager,**

I am excited to apply for the IT VP / CIO / Global IT Head position. With more than 20 years of experience leading global IT operations, infrastructure modernization, digital transformation, and enterprise technology programs, I have built and scaled technology organizations that directly support business growth and operational excellence.

Currently serving as IT Director at Universal Weather & Aviation, I lead enterprise-wide IT strategy and oversee a global portfolio covering infrastructure, operations, security, governance, and digital initiatives. I have successfully built IT organizations from the ground up, expanding teams from 3 to over 30 professionals while managing cross-functional global teams exceeding 70 members.

Throughout my career, I have focused on aligning technology investments with business objectives. I have led strategic programs exceeding $5 million, managed multimillion-dollar IT budgets, negotiated enterprise vendor contracts, and implemented governance frameworks that improve operational efficiency and reduce business risk.

My experience includes:

* Leading global IT infrastructure and service delivery
* Driving digital transformation and process automation
* Managing IT governance, risk, and compliance initiatives
* Building scalable IT operations across multiple geographies
* Implementing ITIL-based service management frameworks
* Managing cybersecurity, asset lifecycle, and vendor ecosystems
* Delivering measurable cost optimization initiatives

Some notable achievements include reducing IT procurement costs by 15%, delivering annual savings through Six Sigma initiatives, optimizing telecom and infrastructure expenses, and establishing new technology operations across multiple locations.

In addition to my technical leadership, I believe my strongest capability is building high-performing teams and creating a culture of accountability, innovation, and continuous improvement. My certifications, including PMP, CISM, AWS Solution Architect, ITIL, and Executive MBA in IT Operations, complement my practical leadership experience.

I am particularly interested in organizations seeking a technology executive who can combine strategic thinking with hands-on execution while enabling business transformation through secure and scalable technology platforms.

I would welcome the opportunity to discuss how my background can contribute to your organization's long-term growth and technology vision.

**Sincerely,**

**Vivek Singh**
